FAQ

Questions owners ask us first

When does your team work?

We’re a fully remote team that works during your overnight hours. Calls happen in your US business day, and work sent in the evening is usually ready by the next morning.

How do you protect my financial data?

Encrypted file portals, multi-factor authentication, role-based access, signed NDAs and a written information security plan. Sensitive data never sits on personal devices.

Can you file my business taxes?

We keep your books tax-ready and prepare year-end packages and 1099 data for your CPA. Returns are signed and filed by a licensed US CPA or EA — yours, or a partner firm we can introduce.

We’re a CPA firm. How do you handle Section 7216?

Your client signs an offshore disclosure consent, you mask SSNs before files leave your system, and we prepare in your software. You stay preparer of record and file under your own credentials.

How do you price your work?

Fixed monthly fees based on transaction volume and services, never more than $1,000 a month. You’ll know the price before we start.
