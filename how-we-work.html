2 · Your team

Four people behind every account — and one person you talk to

Work is prepared by one person and checked by another before it reaches you. That two-step review is how we keep errors out.

1

Client Manager

Your single point of contact. Answers within one business day, sends weekly status updates and delivers your reports.

2

Accounting / Tax Associate

Records and categorizes transactions, reconciles accounts and prepares draft returns in your software.

3

Senior Accountant (Reviewer)

Reviews every close and return, flags unusual items and signs off quality.

4

Founder / Virtual CFO

Quality control across accounts, monthly strategy calls on CFO plans, and the escalation point for anything urgent.

Associate prepares→Senior reviews→Manager delivers→You approve
